Russia, Ukraine and the US ended two days of talks in Abu Dhabi with the biggest prisoner exchange since 2022 and no ceasefire, hours after Moscow fired 163 drones, 91 of them jet-powered, at Ukraine; Ukrainian drones kept Omsk under alert for seven hours (BBC, AP, ISW, Meduza).

US prosecutors charged three Iran-linked operatives over an alleged attack on RAF Fairford and about a dozen plots in the US and Europe (CBS), as Trump said the US had "already won" its war with Iran and wanted "total victory"(Times of Israel); oil hit a two-month low.

Nvidia's board met and hired a law firm over a scandal around chief executive Jensen Huang, including a relationship with a woman alleged to be a Chinese spy (CNN); the $4.2tn company is 6% of the S&P 500, which slipped as October inflation rose 0.4% and the 10-year Treasury yield reached 4.983% (NDTV Profit).

El Salvador's helicopters rocketed a compound in Haiti it called the headquarters of the 400 Mawozo gang; Haiti's prime minister said "hats off to Bukele", and leader Wilson Joseph was not there (BBC).

An Israeli strike hit Nasser Hospital's intensive-care unit in Khan Younis, killing at least six and wounding 20, Gaza's civil defence said (NYT).

Scott Colom won Mississippi's Senate seat, the first Black statewide officeholder there since Reconstruction (CBS), and a House bid to impeach Pete Hegseth failed (BBC).

COP31's leaders' summit opened in Antalya with Recep Tayyip Erdogan and UN chief Antonio Guterres (AFP). Congo's Ebola outbreak passed 11,000 cases and 5,387 deaths (AP). Indonesia's vice-president, Gibran Rakabuming Raka, resigned and President Prabowo Subianto accepted (Reuters). Iran named Argentina's navy and air force terrorist organisations (Al Jazeera).
